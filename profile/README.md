## First Look at SimpleSYN Business

SimpleSYN Business is for people who keep Outlook on two PCs they own and want one named mailbox pair. It gives you a folder desk, an Outlook sync, and a short path back to machines you already run. It does not replace Outlook. It sits beside Outlook and keeps the folders close.

Work stays on PCs you own. Extra machines wait for their own title.
